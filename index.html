About

I'm Sydney. I've designed for government, financial services and consumer apps, and I draw whenever I'm not doing that.

Most recently I was the UX designer on a product team at Deseret First Credit Union, before starting a graduate programme at the London School of Economics. My work runs from interviews, surveys and heat mapping through to personas, journey maps and shipped interfaces, often on tight timelines and inside existing design systems.

I'm looking for UX and product design roles in London. If you'd like to work together, I'd love to hear from you.
