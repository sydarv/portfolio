About

I'm Sydney, a UX designer who has worked across government, financial services and consumer apps. Most recently I was the sole UX designer on a product team at Deseret First Credit Union, before starting a graduate programme at the London School of Economics.

My work runs from user interviews, surveys and heat mapping through to personas, journey maps and shipped interfaces, often on tight timelines and inside existing design systems.
